About the Dataset

Our dataset contains vegetarian and vegan recipes collected manually from different online sources. Each recipe includes the dish name, dietary category, preparation time, allergen information and a link to the original recipe.

Why We Chose This Information

Initially, we considered attributes such as taste and meal type, but both of these were ultimately discarded for various reasons. It was decided that the attribute "taste" was not enough to describe the dishes, as it generally came down to deciding if a dish was sweet or savoury. For this reason we decided to discard the attribute and focus only on savory dishes. The meal type attribute was also discarded, as we figured assigning labels like "dinner" or "breakfast" to meals came down too much to personal preference and cultural differences, which may have been difficult for us to decide.

To narrow down the objective of the project, we decided to focus only on vegan and vegetarian recipes, and we also felt like adding the allergens attribute was relevant, as it would specify the goal of the project further. The preparation time attribute was also deemed relevant, as users are likely to have different needs in this regard. To then make the dataset as reliable as possible, we sourced real life recipes to make up the data. We also made sure to use the same structure for each recipe to keep the information organised and easier to compare.
